Product Description

Ceylon cinnamon, also known as "true cinnamon," is native to Sri Lanka. Unlike its more common cousin, Cassia cinnamon, Ceylon cinnamon has a more delicate, complex flavor profile with sweet and citrusy notes.

Our Ceylon cinnamon sticks are harvested from the inner bark of the Cinnamomum verum tree. The bark is carefully peeled, dried, and rolled into the characteristic quills you see here. The lighter color and thinner, more brittle texture are hallmarks of genuine Ceylon cinnamon.

Ceylon cinnamon contains less coumarin than Cassia cinnamon, making it a healthier choice for regular consumption.

How to Use

Ceylon cinnamon sticks can be used in a variety of ways:

  • Add a stick to hot beverages like tea, coffee, or hot chocolate
  • Use in mulled wine or cider for a warm, spicy flavor
  • Add to simmering rice pudding or oatmeal
  • Include in savory dishes like curries and stews
  • Use as a stirrer for cocktails to add subtle flavor

To release more flavor, gently crush or break the stick before using. Remove the stick before serving.
