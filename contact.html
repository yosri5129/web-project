Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within the continental US. Express shipping options are available at checkout.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to select international destinations. Shipping times and costs vary by location.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Products must be unopened and in their original packaging.

How should I store my spices?

For maximum freshness, store spices in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Our containers are designed to help preserve freshness.
